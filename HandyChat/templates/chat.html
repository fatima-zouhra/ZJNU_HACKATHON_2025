FREQUENT QUESTIONS

What is this app for?

This app is used by students to record weekly running sessions required for Physical Education (PE) classes.

📅 How many times do I need to run each week?

Each student must complete two valid runs per week.

📏 What are the distance requirements?

  • Men: At least 3.5 km per run
  • Women: At least 3 km per run

🗺️ What are the green and orange points?

Each valid run must pass through at least two green checkpoints and one orange checkpoint on the campus map.

🚫 What happens if I don't complete my weekly runs?

If you miss a week or don't meet the distance and checkpoint requirements, your record for that week will be marked as incomplete and may affect your PE grade.

🏫 Can I run anywhere?

No — runs must be completed within the designated university running zones shown in the app's map.

💡 Tips for better results

  • Check your GPS before starting your run.
  • Make sure your phone has enough battery and mobile data.
  • Run during allowed hours and avoid pausing for too long.
